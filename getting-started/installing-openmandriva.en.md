# Installing OpenMandriva

Installing OpenMandriva is quick. Once you have your USB stick ready, the installer guides you through the rest.

{: .important }

Always back up your data before you begin. Installing OpenMandriva may shrink, reformat, or wipe hard-drive partitions.

1. Copy OpenMandriva to a USB stick with at least 8 GB of storage. See [Copying OpenMandriva to a USB Stick](./copying-to-usb.en.html). When finished shut down your computer completely (don't put it to sleep; shut it off). 

1. Boot from the USB stick by inserting the stick and turning your computer on. Some computers boot from the USB stick automatically; most don't. If yours doesn't, press the boot-menu key during startup. Common keys include F12 and ESC; F2 and F10 also work. Most computers show the correct key while the vendor logo is displayed.

1. Start the live session. Live mode runs a full OpenMandriva session from the USB stick without modifying your hard drive. Use it to test the system before installing.

   ![Choose the live session (highlighted).](./images/installing-openmandriva-01.png) 

1. Close OpenMandriva Welcome; you don't need it right now. Open the installer, *Calamares*. From the live desktop, click the *Install OpenMandriva Lx* icon.

   ![Click the Install OpenMandriva icon.](./images/installing-openmandriva-02.png) 

1. Choose your language on the first page, then click *Next*.

   ![Choose your language and keyboard layout.](./images/installing-openmandriva-03.png) 

1. Choose your location by clicking on the world map, and then click *Next*. 

   ![Choose your location.](./images/installing-openmandriva-04.png) 

1. Choose your keyboard layout. Usually, the right keyboard is selected by default. When you've made your choice, click *Next*. 

   ![Choose your keyboard layout.](./images/installing-openmandriva-05.png) 

1. Now it's time to partition your drive. Calamares offers three options:

   - **Install alongside an existing operating system** — keep your current OS and add OpenMandriva. This suits most users.

   - **Erase disk and install OpenMandriva** — reclaim the whole drive. This removes everything else.

   - **Format a partition and install** — control which partition holds OpenMandriva.

You may want to pause here, read [Partitioning](./partitioning/index.en.html), and decide how you want to partition your machine. When you have decided, make your choices here. What's pictured below is an example, not a recommendation. Choose how to partition your drive and click *Next*.

   ![Partition your drive.](./images/installing-openmandriva-06.png) 

1. Create a user account and set a password. 

   ![Create an account, name your machine, and set a password.](./images/installing-openmandriva-07.png) 

1. The installer next shows a summary of all the choices you've made. If you want to go back and make a different choice, click *Back* to make your change. Nothing happens to your machine until you click the Install button. When you're ready, click *Install*. 

1. For extra safety, a dialog box appears, giving you another chance to go back or install now. When you click *Install Now*, you are past the point of no return; changes are made to your machine to install OpenMandriva. 

   ![When you click Install Now, you are past the point of no return.](./images/installing-openmandriva-08.png) 

Now all you have to do is sit back and watch the installation happen. OpenMandriva usually installs in only minutes. When it's finished, check the box to restart and click *Done*. 

![When the installation is complete, you can restart your computer.](./images/installing-openmandriva-10.png) 

OpenMandriva then boots on your system. Provide the password you set to log in, and the desktop appears. You're ready to go! 

![The OpenMandriva desktop appears. ](./images/installing-openmandriva-11.png)
