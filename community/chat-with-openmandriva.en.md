# Chat with OpenMandriva

Chat is often the fastest way to get help or talk with the OpenMandriva community. Anyone can join, and newcomers are welcome.

## Where to Find Us on Matrix

OpenMandriva rooms live in the official Matrix space. Open it in a Matrix client such as [Element](https://app.element.io/#/room/#openmandriva:matrix.org), or [open the Matrix space](https://matrix.to/#/#openmandriva-space:matrix.org) directly.

- **OpenMandriva** (`#openmandriva:matrix.org`), the main channel for getting help, asking questions, and chatting with other users.
- **Chat** (`#openmandriva-chat:matrix.org`), off-topic conversation and general discussion.
- **Cooker** (`#openmandriva-cooker:matrix.org`), for work and technical talk with developers. This is where developers work, so mind your manners.

## Getting Started

Set up a Matrix account before joining, then add yourself to one of the channels above. If you need a hand, message the `#openmandriva` channel.

## Before You Ask Something

- People are in different time zones, and the team is small, so it can take a while to get a reply.
- Explain what you did, what you expected, and what actually happened. Include anything that might help someone help you, like the version you are using.
- Do not repeatedly message the same person for the same request.

## Before Changing a Channel

If something feels wrong with a channel's behavior, do not change it without discussing it with the team first. Explain what you plan to change, why you plan to change it, and ask for feedback before proceeding.
