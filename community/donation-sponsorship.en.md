# Donation

OpenMandriva is built by volunteers who work in their free time. The project is not a commercial organization and does not pay for work, but it does cost money to keep the servers running.

## How to Help

You can support OpenMandriva in several ways---with your skills or with a donation.

### Contribute Your Skills

The development teams---Cooker and QA---always need new contributors to help create and maintain packages, fix bugs, and test releases. Contributing is rewarding and a lot of fun.

If coding is not your strength, the Workshop group (artwork, documentation, translation, and communication teams) welcomes your contributions. Read the [Contributing to OpenMandriva](https://openmandriva.org/get-involved) page for details on how to join.

### Donate Money

If you can, [donate](https://www.openmandriva.org/donate) to keep the servers running and the project alive.
