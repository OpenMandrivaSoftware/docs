# Release Codenames

OpenMandriva assigns a codename to each release. Codenames are single words, each starting with a letter that follows the alphabetical sequence from the beginning.

## Codename List

Here is the list of codenames for OpenMandriva releases starting from 2013.0:

- **O**xygen --- OMLx 2013.0
- **P**hosphorus --- OMLx 2014.0
- **E**insteinium --- OMLx 3.0
- **N**itrogen --- OMLx 4.0
- **M**ercury --- OMLx 4.1
- **A**rgon --- OMLx 4.2
- **N**ickel --- Cooker
- **D**ysprosium --- OMLx 4.3
- **R**ome --- Rolling release
- **I**odine --- OMLx 5.0
- **V**anadium --- OMLx 6.0
- **A**urum --- upcoming
