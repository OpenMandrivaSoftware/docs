# Governance

The OpenMandriva Association *(OMA)* runs through a number of organs.

The Council outlines the overall purpose, structure, and processes for OMA. The [Statutes](./statutes.en.html) and [Bylaws](./index.en.html) of OMA document the Association's policies and procedures as a non-profit organization under French law.

## The Council

The Council tends to meet twice a month to discuss regular activities, and publishes an annual activity and financial report. The current council members are listed on the [About page](../about-openmandriva/index.en.html). The Chairman is Bernhard Rosenkränzer, the Secretary is Kate Lebedeff, and the Treasurer is Jean-Claude Vanier.

## Technical Committee

The Technical Committee (TC) manages the technical aspects of the Association projects and leads the OpenMandriva Lx project. It is part of the Advisory Board and is the core technical organ of the Association. The TC holds weekly meetings; the log is [here](https://chwido.openmandriva.org/meetings/%23openmandriva-cooker/), and the meetings are announced on the [forum](https://forum.openmandriva.org/t/events-and-meetings-calendar/2735). The TC coordinator is Bernhard Rosenkränzer.

## Advisory Board

The Advisory Board gathers sponsors of OMA (at the platinum level), the Users Delegate, the Technical Committee, and external consultants. It has no decision-making authority, but it helps guide the overall direction of the Association.

## Members Assembly

The Members Assembly consists of all OMA members and contributors. Members can run for election to the Council, vote, suggest referendums, and even dissolve the Association, and it supervises the Council's activities.

## Membership Committee and User Delegate

The Membership Committee oversees membership acceptance and manages memberships. The User Delegate is elected by the community for two years and works with the Council, bringing community wishes to the Association and acting as a core contact for community members.
