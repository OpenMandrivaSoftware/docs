Auto-included by the Just-the-Docs `head.html` include. - Anti-Flicker (FOUT) inline script: resolves the color-scheme preference (saved choice in localStorage — 'auto'/'dark'/'light', falling back to the OS `prefers-color-scheme` media query) and swaps the Just-the-Docs per-scheme stylesheet (`just-the-docs-{light,dark}.css`) *before* the first paint, so the page never flashes with the wrong scheme. Also exposes `data-theme` (the scheme to compile colors with) and `data-mode` (auto/dark/light, drives the toggle's icon) on , both reused by the external theme-toggle.js. - Deferred external script that wires up the toggle button's behaviour. {%- endcomment -%}
